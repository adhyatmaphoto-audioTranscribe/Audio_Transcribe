Yajnah, let your life be a life of sacrifice, every breath be offering, and practice pancha mahayajna, five modes of great rituals: prayer to God, study of scriptures and discussing scriptural values with your friends or family members, serve your guest, guest is God, serve your parents, and serve the humanity, at the same time, the nature. Svadhyayah, study the scriptures, understand the scriptures, study yourself, try to live in knowledge. Tapah, prayer, meditation, staying in Ajna chakra. Arjavam, simplicity, straightforwardness.
