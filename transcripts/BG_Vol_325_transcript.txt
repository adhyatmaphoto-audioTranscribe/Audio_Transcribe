There is a verse which is often quote in the yogic textbooks, सूर्यमध्यस्थितः सोमः सोममध्ये हुताशनः। तेजोमध्ये स्थितं सत्त्वं सत्त्वमध्ये स्थितोऽच्युतः॥ सूर्यमध्यस्थितः सोमः, inside the sun, you'll find there is presence of moon. Inside the moon, you'll find the presence of fire. Inside the fire, there, you'll find the presence of stillness. Inside that stillness, you'll find the presence of अच्युत, undeviated, steady divine. When you concentrate and go within and experiencing inner light, through ज्योतिमुद्रा or योनिमुद्रा, in that state, you, one might see the golden ring, that is the sun. Inside the golden ring, there is little darkness and little light, it is moon. Inside that in the center there is a bright star like it is fire. If you go to the center of that star, you'll find complete stillness. And when you enter into the center of stillness, you'll find the experience of the divine, अच्युत। सूर्यमध्यस्थितः सोमः सोममध्ये हुताशनः तेजोमध्ये स्थितं सत्त्वं सत्त्वमध्ये स्थितोऽच्युतः। Arjuna, whatever you see, whatever you feel, it is all me, and I am in the center of everything.
Through meditation, constant effort, discriminative attitude, you can experience it, which the Lord told in the previous verse, यतन्तो योगिनः। I'll be a yogi, I'll be putting constant effort. In every breath, I will try, I'll not ignore, I'll not neglect, I'll not waste my precious time and beautiful opportunity.
