And if you look at this last verse, when he is telling, "O Anagha," Anagha means it is coming from agh dhatu, a-g-h. Agh dhatu, agh dhatu means to do wrong, to commit mistake, to sin, agh. One who is not doing any mistake, one who is free from sins, one who is beyond wrong, he is anagha, the sinless, the pure one. It is said one who is anagha, one who is free from desires, vasana shunya, one who is free from asuya, is free from jealousy, one who is free from dosha drishti, one who is not looking at the mistake or fault of others, means one who is trying to perceive divinity everywhere, anagha. O Anagha, in this manner, guhyatamam idam shastram maya uktam, I explained near you the most secret and the most profound wisdom. Guhyatamam, guhya means the secret. Why it is secret? Because you cannot know until and unless it is revealed near you. Why it is secret? Because it has been concealed, you have no way to know it. Guhyatamam, whatever is visible, it is below the eyebrows. Above the eyebrows, even with eyes open I can't see. Below the eyebrows, I can look at my face to some extent. Similarly, whatever is visible, it is below the eyebrows means in the chakras. Whatever is visible in this world, if I want to know who am I, I have to withdraw myself from all the chakras and to go to Kutastha where it is remaining in secret. It is Kutastha, kuta means secret. Guhya, guhyatara, guhyatama. Idam guhyatamam shastram. The simple translation of shastra is scripture, but here it is not a shastra is holding a scripture and telling that, "This is this, the secret knowledge, you take it." Here shastra means the teaching. Why we will tell teaching? Because he told maya ukta, I have told. Shastra doesn't mean a book. Shastra means the disciplined, the topic of self-discipline, shasanat shastram, shastram yadyapi Gitakhyam samastam shastram uchyate. So he is telling that, "These teachings of mine is the most secret one." Etat buddhva buddhiman syat. You want to be the most intelligent one? The most intelligent one, one who knows this. Here the Lord gave the definition of the intelligent people. Once Swami Chinmayananda, he was facing the journalists. He was exceptionally smart, very intelligent, logical, and somebody asked, "Swamiji, you are so intelligent, why don't you join politics and take the leadership of this country?" He said, "The most intelligent people seek for God. The most intelligent people seek for God." Chatur se jana, it is said by Sri Chaitanya, "Je jana Hari bhaje se jana chatur." One who loves God is the most intelligent one. And remember, loving God is not idleness, again to remind you, it is not the business of lazy people. One who can love God, one who has that strength within to overcome all weaknesses, to establish relationship with the beloved, etat buddhva buddhiman syat, one who knows this is the most intelligent one, and when one knows this means knowing Kshara, Akshara, and Purushottama, perishable, imperishable, and the supreme being, visible world, invisible spirit, and beyond visible and invisible, the cosmic being, body, the knower of the body, and beyond, one who knows this is the most intelligent one. Kritakrityah, Gurudev used to tell, "Dhanyo 'ham, kritakrityo 'ham, saphalam jeevanam mama." It is in Vivekachudamani also. "Dhanyo 'ham," I feel feel myself blessed. "Kritakrityo 'ham," I feel myself complete and content. Kritakrityah, kritam krityam kartavyam yena sa kritakrityah. Kritam krityam, kritam means having done, krityam means what is to be done. This word kritakrityah is beautiful. Kritam means having done, I have done. What you have done? What is to be done? What is to be done has been done by me, I have nothing more to gain, nothing more to lose, whatever I have to do, I have done it. Can anybody come to this state to tell that, "I am complete, I am full, I am completely satisfied, I have nothing more to do." Kritam krityam, kritam means having done, krityam means what is to be done, kritakrityah, one who has accomplished all duties. Iti guhyatamam shastram idam ukta mayanagha etat buddhva buddhiman syat kritakrityashcha bharata. O Bharata, O lover of light, O Arjuna, O Anagha, sinless, pure, stainless, O Arjuna, I narrated near you the secret, the most secret teaching, one who knows this is the most intelligent one, one who lives with this has accomplished everything, nothing more to be known, nothing more to be done. This is the end of the 15th chapter. The essence of the 15th chapter, it is to know the Lord as Purushottama, to know the Lord as the cosmic being. In this chapter, few things have been described, I will just tell in brief. To know this world in essence, or to know this body. Knowing this world and knowing this body, to surrender to God, it is in the verse 4, tameva chadya purusham prapadye, I surrender near you, O the primordial one, the adi purusha. Then to know, to experience divinity within oneself, it is in the verse 11, pashyanti atmany avasthitam. One is to go beyond the knowledge of scriptures to experience within, the verse 15. To love God, to serve God in every possible way, in the verse 19, and the 20th is the sec- the most secret wisdom, thus comes the end of 15th chapter.
